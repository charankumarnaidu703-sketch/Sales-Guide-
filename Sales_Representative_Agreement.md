# INDEPENDENT SALES REPRESENTATIVE AGREEMENT

---

## RECITALS

**WHEREAS**, the Company designs, develops, and delivers high-ticket digital storefronts and AI-powered operations software for the landscaping and outdoor living industry; and

**WHEREAS**, the Company desires to retain the services of the Representative to identify, qualify, and secure potential clients for its proprietary products, specifically **Verdant** and **Chattie**; and

**WHEREAS**, the Representative has represented that they possess the necessary skills, expertise, and resources to perform such sales activities and wishes to perform these services as an independent contractor under the terms and conditions set forth herein.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration for the mutual promises and covenants contained in this Agreement, the parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. SCOPE OF SERVICES & RESPONSIBILITIES

The Representative is engaged as an Independent Sales Representative. The primary objective is to prospect and identify potential clients who have a genuine need for, and interest in, the Company’s products.

### Core Duties
1. **Lead Generation & Qualification:** Prospect, cold contact (via email, phone, or messaging platforms), and pre-qualify high-value commercial and residential landscaping/stonemasonry businesses.
2. **Needs Assessment:** Identify businesses that either lack a professional digital presence or suffer from severe operational lead leaks (e.g., missed calls, delayed responses).
3. **Product Positioning:** Articulate the value proposition of **Verdant** and **Chattie** to qualified business owners and decision-makers, guiding them through the initial interest phase.
4. **Transition to Closing:** Secure qualified interest and smoothly transition warm leads to the Principal for contract finalization and technical onboarding.

## 3. WORKING HOURS & OPERATIONAL AUTONOMY

*   **Flexible Working Hours:** The Representative shall enjoy full autonomy over their working hours and schedule. There are no daily minimum hour requirements. The Representative is free to conduct sales activities at times that align with their personal schedule and the optimal outreach hours of target prospects.
*   **Location of Work:** The Representative may perform the services from any location of their choosing, utilizing their own equipment, software, and communication tools.

---

## 4. REPORTING & COMMUNICATION PROTOCOLS

To maintain structural alignment and track sales pipeline progress, the following reporting cadence is established:

*   **Weekly Sunday Progress Report:** The Representative must submit a formal summary report to the Company every **Sunday**. This report shall detail:
    *   Total outreach volume (calls made, emails sent, direct messages initiated).
    *   List of warm leads, qualified prospects, and their respective follow-up statuses.
    *   Friction points, market feedback, and competitive intelligence.
    *   Planned outreach pipeline for the upcoming week.
*   **Real-Time Breakthrough Notification:** Notwithstanding the weekly Sunday reporting requirement, the Representative is authorized and encouraged to notify the Principal of any significant lead generation, hot prospect, or business breakthrough **at any time (24/7)** to ensure immediate closing action.

---

## 5. COMMISSION & COMPENSATION STRUCTURE

The Representative shall be compensated strictly on a commission-only basis, calculated as follows:

| Commission Category | Rate | Terms of Payment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Initial Project Share** | **30%** | Calculated on the gross contract value of any project closed as a direct result of a lead generated and qualified by the Representative. Paid within seven (7) business days of client payment clearance. |
| **Monthly Recurring Commission** | **30%** | Applied to recurring subscription, hosting, or support packages associated with accounts brought in by the Representative. Paid monthly within seven (7) business days of the client’s monthly payment clearance, for as long as the contract remains active. |

### Compensation Details
*   **No Base Salary:** The Representative acknowledges and agrees that no base salary, draw, or hourly wage is provided under this Agreement.
*   **Expense Responsibility:** The Representative shall be solely responsible for all operating expenses incurred during their sales activities (including phone charges, travel, internet, and marketing tools), unless pre-approved in writing by the Company.

---

## 6. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR RELATIONSHIP

The parties agree that the Representative is performing services solely as an **Independent Contractor**. 
*   **No Employment Status:** Nothing in this Agreement shall construct an employer-employee, partnership, or joint venture relationship between the Company and the Representative.
*   **Tax Liabilities:** The Representative is solely responsible for all federal, state, local, or self-employment taxes, and any registrations or insurances required by law in their jurisdiction. The Company will not withhold any taxes from commission payments.

---

## 7. TERM & TERMINATION

*   **At-Will Term:** This Agreement shall begin on the Effective Date and remain in effect until terminated by either party.
*   **Termination Notice:** Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, by providing **seven (7) days written notice** to the other party.
*   **Post-Termination Commissions:** Upon termination, the Representative shall remain entitled to receive commissions on any pending projects they qualified that close within thirty (30) days post-termination, as well as recurring commissions for a period of thirty (30) days, after which all commission obligations cease.
